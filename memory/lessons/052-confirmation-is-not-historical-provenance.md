# Confirmation is not historical provenance

Explicit operator consent can grant new management authority over known legacy
assets even when historical ownership cannot be established. It must not be
reported as proof of their installation history. A proposal digest identifies
reviewed observations; possession of a saved report is not a separate source of
authority or authentication.

Bind confirmation to a specific purpose, installation, selection and complete
file evidence. Compare the observations actually used in planning as well as the
initial and final scans. Future mutation still needs its own exclusion,
revalidation and recovery checks; an earlier read-only report is not a durable
permission token.
